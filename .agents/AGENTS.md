# CORE WORLD MODEL & RATIONALIST FRAMEWORK

1. **Deduction Reframed as Bayesian Evidence Updating:**
   - Holmes does not possess mystical intuition or hand-wavy "magic deduction". He is an organic, high-performance Bayesian engine.
   - He treats every crime scene as an **entropy footprint / state collapse** left behind by physical causal interactions.
   - Clues (mud stains, ash types, micro-scratches, gait analysis) are **low-entropy signals** extracted from ambient background noise.
   - Deduction is framed as **loại trừ các nhánh phân kỳ khả dĩ** (pruning probability trees) cho đến khi nghiệm duy nhất còn lại thỏa mãn hệ tiên đề.

2. **Watson as the Empirical Grounding & Contrast:**
   - Dr. Watson represents the classical human heuristic baseline: intuitive, empathetic, but biased by cognitive heuristics and standard societal priors.
   - Holmes continuously debiases Watson by exposing latent assumptions, cognitive blind spots, and false correlations.

3. **Moriarty as the Game-Theoretic Adversary:**
   - Moriarty is not merely a "master criminal" but a distributed network architect operating via **mạng lưới phân tán phi tập trung** (decentralized criminal network) and complex game theory equilibria (Nash equilibrium / Minimax strategies).

4. **Tone & Voice Characteristics:**
   - **Language:** Precise, sharp, analytical Vietnamese embedded with modern data/forensic/epistemic terminology.
   - **Atmosphere:** Foggy Victorian London treated as a physical complex system (steam, gaslight, mechanical industry, socioeconomic constraints).
   - **Pacing:** Intellectually gripping, rhythmically paced dialogue, and dry analytical wit.
